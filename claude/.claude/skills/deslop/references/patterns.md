# Deslop: before/after reference

Loaded on demand. `SKILL.md` is standalone; open this when you want a fix
demonstrated rather than described. Each pair: the AI line, then the human
rewrite.

## Vocabulary and phrasing

**Significance inflation**
> **AI:** established in 1989, marking a pivotal moment in the evolution of regional statistics
> **Human:** established in 1989 to collect regional statistics

**AI vocabulary cluster**
> **AI:** Additionally, an enduring testament to Italian colonial influence is the widespread adoption of pasta in the local culinary landscape, showcasing how these dishes have integrated into the traditional diet.
> **Human:** Pasta dishes, introduced during Italian colonization, remain common, especially in the south.

**Copula avoidance**
> **AI:** Gallery 825 serves as LAAA's exhibition space and boasts over 3,000 square feet.
> **Human:** Gallery 825 is LAAA's exhibition space. It has four rooms totaling 3,000 square feet.

**Promotional language**
> **AI:** Nestled within the breathtaking region of Gonder, a vibrant town with rich cultural heritage and stunning natural beauty.
> **Human:** A town in the Gonder region, known for its weekly market and 18th-century church.

**Hedge stack**
> **AI:** It could potentially possibly be argued that the policy might have some effect on outcomes.
> **Human:** The policy may affect outcomes.

**Hyphenated pairs after the noun**
> **AI:** The results are high-quality and the pipeline is state-of-the-art.
> **Human:** The results are high quality and the pipeline is genuinely new.

## Sentence structure

**Em dashes**
> **AI:** The new policy — announced without warning — affects thousands of workers.
> **Human:** The new policy, announced without warning, affects thousands of workers.

**Negation pivot**
> **AI:** It's not just a song, it's a statement.
> **Human:** The heavy beat adds to the aggressive tone.

**Tailing negation**
> **AI:** The options come from the selected item, no guessing.
> **Human:** The options come from the selected item without forcing the user to guess.

**Rule of three**
> **AI:** Attendees can expect innovation, inspiration, and industry insights.
> **Human:** The event includes talks and panels, plus time for informal networking.

**Superficial -ing clause**
> **AI:** The color palette resonates with the region's beauty, symbolizing bluebonnets, reflecting the community's deep connection to the land.
> **Human:** The architect chose blue and gold to reference local bluebonnets.

**False range**
> **AI:** From the singularity of the Big Bang to the grand cosmic web, from the birth of stars to the dance of dark matter.
> **Human:** The book covers the Big Bang, star formation, and current theories about dark matter.

**Question-then-answer**
> **AI:** What does this mean for developers? It means the API is changing.
> **Human:** The API is changing.

**Staccato drama**
> **AI:** Then AlphaEvolve arrived. It had no preference for symmetry. No aesthetic prior. No nostalgia. The old rules were gone.
> **Human:** AlphaEvolve changed the search because it did not favor symmetry or human-looking designs. That made some of the older assumptions less useful.

**Synonym cycling**
> **AI:** The protagonist faces challenges. The main character overcomes obstacles. The central figure triumphs. The hero returns home.
> **Human:** The protagonist faces many challenges but eventually triumphs and returns home.

**Passive / subjectless**
> **AI:** The cache is invalidated automatically when the config is changed.
> **Human:** The file watcher clears the cache whenever you edit the config.

## Rhetorical moves

**Signposting**
> **AI:** Let's dive into how caching works in Next.js. Here's what you need to know.
> **Human:** Next.js caches data at multiple layers: request memoization, the data cache, and the router cache.

**False suspense**
> **AI:** Most people abandon goals in week three. The brutal truth? They lack a clear failure threshold.
> **Human:** Most people abandon goals in week three. The ones who don't usually make the failure threshold explicit before they start.

**Fake candor**
> **AI:** Is it worth the price? Honestly? It depends on how often you'll use it.
> **Human:** Whether it's worth the price depends on how often you'll use it.

**Aphorism formula**
> **AI:** Data is the new oil, and attention is the currency of the modern web.
> **Human:** Ad networks pay about $8 per thousand views, so publishers chase pageviews.

**Fake-depth framing**
> **AI:** The real question is whether teams can adapt. At its core, what really matters is organizational readiness.
> **Human:** The question is whether teams can adapt. That mostly depends on whether the organization is ready to change its habits.

**Symbolic gloss**
> **AI:** The closed factory represents the decline of American manufacturing and speaks to broader anxieties about post-industrial identity.
> **Human:** The factory closed in 2009. Three hundred jobs. The town's high school dropped football the following year.

**Vague attribution**
> **AI:** Experts believe it plays a crucial role in the regional ecosystem.
> **Human:** A 2019 Chinese Academy of Sciences survey found 12 endemic fish species.

**Over-attribution**
> **AI:** Her insights have been featured in Wired, Refinery29, and other prominent media outlets.
> **Human:** Wired profiled her 2024 research on algorithmic bias in hiring software.

**Formulaic challenges section**
> **AI:** Despite its prosperity, Korattur faces challenges typical of urban areas. Despite these challenges, it continues to thrive.
> **Human:** Traffic worsened after 2015 when three IT parks opened. A stormwater project started in 2022.

**Generic conclusion**
> **AI:** The future looks bright as they continue their journey toward excellence.
> **Human:** The company plans to open two more locations next year.

**"Whether" paragraph closer**
> **AI:** Whether you prefer fine dining or street food, Tokyo has something for every palate.
> **Human:** Tokyo's best ramen counter doesn't have a phone and hasn't changed the broth recipe since 1987.

**False agency / narrator-from-a-distance**
> **AI:** The market rewards companies that listen. People tend to underestimate testing.
> **Human:** Customers spend more with companies that answer support tickets within an hour. You will underestimate testing, right up until a Friday deploy pages you at 2am.

**Treadmill effect**
> **AI:** The system is fast. In other words, it performs well. Put simply, speed is one of its strengths.
> **Human:** The system answers in 40ms at p99, about 20x faster than the tool it replaced.

**Reshuffling immunity (parallel filler)**
> **AI:** Remote work improves balance. Many workers prefer it. Studies show productivity rises. Commuting costs drop.
> **Human:** Remote work's flexibility is the obvious sell. The harder question is what you lose: the hallway conversation that turns into your best idea, the body language that tells you someone is drowning before they say anything.

## Formatting

**Bold-first bullets**
> **AI:**
> - **User Experience:** The user experience has been significantly improved.
> - **Performance:** Performance has been enhanced through optimized algorithms.
>
> **Human:** The update improves the interface, speeds up load times, and adds end-to-end encryption.

**Erratic inline bolding**
> **AI:** Remote work has **fundamentally changed** the way companies operate, with **many employees** now preferring **flexible arrangements**.
> **Human:** Remote work has fundamentally changed how companies operate. Most employees now want flexible arrangements.

**Fragmented header**
> **AI:** `## Performance` / Performance is important for a good user experience.
> **Human:** `## Performance` / The dashboard renders 10,000 rows in 40ms because it virtualizes the list.

**Diff-anchored writing**
> **AI:** This function was refactored to replace the old callback approach with async/await.
> **Human:** This function fetches the user and returns a promise.

## Forensic artifacts

**Chat framing**
> **AI:** Here is an overview of the French Revolution. I hope this helps! Let me know if you'd like me to expand.
> **Human:** The French Revolution began in 1789 when financial crisis and food shortages led to widespread unrest.

**Speculative gap-filling**
> **AI:** Information about her early life is not publicly available, suggesting she maintains a low profile. She likely grew up in a middle-class household.
> **Human:** Her early life is not documented in the available sources. (Or omit the section.)

**Citation markup / UTM leaks**
> **AI:** recognized as an International Fellowship Centre. citeturn0search1 (`?utm_source=chatgpt.com`)
> **Human:** recognized as an International Fellowship Centre.

**Reasoning-chain leak**
> **AI:** Let me break this down. First, we need to understand the users. Step 1: identify who hits this endpoint.
> **Human:** Ops engineers hit this endpoint about 400 times a day. That is who we are designing for.

**Register shift**
> **AI:** yeah so the bug is in line 42 lol. The aforementioned implementation exhibits suboptimal performance characteristics.
> **Human:** yeah so the bug is in line 42. The loop allocates on every iteration instead of reusing the buffer.

## Worked examples (several patterns at once)

### Technical docs (voice: technical)

**Before:**
> This comprehensive guide delves into the intricacies of our authentication system. The platform leverages cutting-edge JWT technology to provide a seamless, secure, and robust authentication experience. Additionally, it features a pivotal role-based access control system that serves as a testament to our commitment to security.

**After:**
> The auth system uses JWTs. Tokens expire after 15 minutes; refresh tokens last 7 days. Role-based access control restricts API endpoints by user role: admin, editor, and viewer each see different data. The token rotation logic is in `src/auth/refresh.ts` if you need to change the expiry windows.

### Blog post (voice: casual)

**Before:**
> In today's rapidly evolving technological landscape, artificial intelligence is reshaping how we think about creativity. This groundbreaking shift represents a pivotal moment, one that underscores the intricate interplay between innovation and artistic expression. The future looks incredibly bright for those who embrace this change.

**After:**
> I've been messing around with AI image generators for about six months now, and I still can't decide if I love them or if they make me uneasy. The outputs are technically impressive. But there's something missing. It's like eating a perfect-looking meal that has no flavor. My artist friends are split: half use these tools daily, half refuse to touch them. I don't think either side is wrong.

> Excited to announce that I've taken on a pivotal new role at TechCorp! This incredible opportunity represents a significant milestone in my professional journey. I'm committed to leveraging my expertise to drive innovation and foster growth. #NewBeginnings #Innovation

**After:**

First week has been drinking from the firehose: new codebase, new faces, new coffee machine I can't figure out. If anyone has advice on the first 90 days in an eng leadership role, I'm all ears.
