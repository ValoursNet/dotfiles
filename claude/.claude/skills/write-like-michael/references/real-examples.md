# Real email examples, with identifying details removed

These are authentic passages from the local sent-email export, not synthetic rewrites. Recipient names and the sender signature are replaced with placeholders. Headers, subject lines, dates, recipients, message IDs, and source links are omitted. Excerpts were selected to avoid contact details, named employers or clients, addresses, financial details, and distinctive private circumstances.

Wording and punctuation are preserved from the cleaned export, except for the labelled identity substitutions. Paragraph wrapping has been normalised. “Excerpt” means only the indicated passage is included; nothing has been added to bridge omitted text. Learn the cadence and choices, not the circumstances. Do not copy placeholders into a completed draft when the current request supplies the names. Historical quirks are evidence, not instructions to introduce typos.

## Explaining a technical commercial tradeoff

Excerpt.

> The tech I would be using is industry standard tools, so if I suddenly disappear you wouldn’t have any issues replacing me or handing it over. I can list them out but I imagine they won't mean much.
>
> If the price is too high I can rope in a more junior Software Engineer and ask them to do the bulk of the work for a lower price. Then perhaps just spend a bit of time on my end to ensure it's half decent and on track. I have had this go very well and very badly in the past so we should talk it through if you want to go this route.

These two consecutive body paragraphs show plain explanations, flexibility, and an honest warning about an alternative.
